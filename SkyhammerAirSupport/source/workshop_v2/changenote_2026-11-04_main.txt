AC-130 Gunner Seat
- Viewpoint 0.1.7: leaving the seat no longer throws you back into the isometric view. The seat now switches Viewpoint's view straight to isometric and back (with 0.1.7's camera move) instead of stepping through third person, so you come back in the view you had, and Viewpoint's saved third-person setting is left alone. Viewpoint 0.1.6 works the same way.
- 25mm: no impact sound and no barrel spin any more; the "brrrr" stream plays at full level from the first round.
Roaming Survivors
- Roaming Survivors people are no longer hit by air strikes unless they are hostile to you. The gunner seat still hits anyone, as before.
