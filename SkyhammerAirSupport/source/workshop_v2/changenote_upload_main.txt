AC-130 Gunner Seat
- New night entry video, played from 22:00 to 6:00 with its own sound.
- 40mm rounds hit a slightly wider area.
- Each gun now always opens at its own zoom: after a quick weapon switch the view no longer stays at the previous gun's zoom.
- Fixed infected targeting and attacking the hidden camera while using the gunner seat.

- Blasts and fallout at the camera position no longer count as damage to your body. False camera hit warnings are suppressed, while blast damage at your body's actual position still triggers an alert.
