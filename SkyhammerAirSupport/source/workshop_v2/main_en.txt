[h1]The sky has your back.[/h1]
Call an [b]AH-64 Apache[/b], an [b]A-10 Warthog[/b], an [b]AC-130 gunship[/b], a flight of [b]F-22 Raptors[/b] and a [b]B-2 bomber[/b] over the radio, once you have earned them. Every call is voiced and subtitled, targets are marked on a thermal sensor map, and the aircraft fly overhead in 3D.

[b]Single player only.[/b] Build 42.21+ (tested on 42.22).
For [b]multiplayer[/b], or to play without ZombieBuddy and Viewpoint, use the [b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813973682]Vanilla Edition[/url][/b].

[h1]How to use[/h1]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/TqwXqmRb/main-05-terminal.jpg[/img]
[olist]
[*]Turn on the [b]air support radio[/b] and keep it on you.
[*]Press [b]K[/b] or right-click the radio to open the terminal.
[*]Pick a tab and press [b]CALL[/b].
[*]For the 105mm, the A-10, the F-22 and the B-2, mark the target on the sensor map or in the game view ([b]EYES-ON[/b]).
[/olist]

[h2]Valkyrie Two-Six - A-10[/h2]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/V4fXjQD/m2.jpg[/img]
[list]
[*][b]GAU-8 gun runs[/b]: mark one point and the pilot strafes a 40-tile line through it, in the direction that catches the most infected, never near you.
[*]Between runs, mark a new point or it flies the line back.
[*][b]Napalm[/b] on the same sortie: mark a point and a 40-tile strip goes up in flames.
[/list]

[h2]Banshee Two-One - AC-130[/h2]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/wNyYw8YS/m3.jpg[/img]
[list]
[*][b]40mm[/b]: fires on its own at the infected around you, or where you aim it. Hold and resume fire from the terminal.
[*][b]105mm[/b]: drops shells where you mark.
[*][b]Gunner seat[/b] (single player): fire 25mm/40mm/105mm (keys 1/2/3), with a drifting thermal view and visible tracers. Targets under roofs are hidden.
[/list]

[h2]Raven One-One - F-22[/h2]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/twkR6wMB/m4.jpg[/img]
[list]
[*][b]Flight strike[/b]: mark one point and ten F-22s streak over at Mach speed, 0.4 s apart, dropping 20 heavy bombs on a 70x40-tile strip, in the direction that catches the most infected.
[*]Afterburners, roars, sonic booms and screen shake. Vehicles in the strip are wrecked.
[/list]

[h2]Titan Zero-One - B-2[/h2]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/kgwbVF0b/m5.jpg[/img]
[list]
[*][b]Heavy bombs[/b] carpet the area you mark.
[*]The blasts draw infected from far away.
[*][b]Tactical nuke[/b] (pick it with the weapon button before calling): mark a spot 60-300 tiles away; after 15 s, infected within 140 tiles die. Building destruction within 50 tiles requires Craters in single player.
[/list]

[h2]Fallout[/h2]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/jPXGhQW1/fallout-small-en.png[/img]
[list]
[*]The nuke's ground zero stays radioactive for days. The longer you stay the sicker you get, and it can kill you even after you get out.
[*]Wear a hazmat suit or a gas mask with a filter. Prussian Blue capsules flush it out faster.
[*]NPCs get sick too.
[/list]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Timers use [b]real time[/b]; single-player pause stops them.
[*]Conventional shots and blasts only hit infected [b]outdoors[/b] (unless "Blasts reach indoors" is on). The B-2's bombs go through roofs, up to three floors.
[*]Friendly [b]Bandits[/b] and [b]A-Life[/b] NPCs are never hit by called-in support and show in green on the sensor map; the gunner seat's own guns can hit them.
[*]Tested alongside about 80 mods without observed conflicts.
[/list]

[h1]Killstreak mode[/h1]
[b]Points, not cooldowns[/b]: every infected you kill yourself is a point (air support kills earn none), and points call the aircraft (Apache 80, A-10 120, AC-130 150, F-22 200, B-2 300, nuke 600). Recall before arrival for a refund. Turn it on in the sandbox or Mod Options.

[h1]Earn your air support[/h1]
New-game quest ([b]Air support unlock[/b]): build one [b]relay[/b]: wall satellite dish + military ham radio within 6 tiles + generator power. Right-click the dish, then call Aegis Control on the [b]air support radio[/b] to unlock all five aircraft.
[img]https://i.ibb.co/Y7N1j60z/relay-420.jpg[/img]
Press [b]K[/b] for the quest log. Don't want the quest? Pick [b]Any two-way radio[/b] in the sandbox.

[h1]FAQ[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Existing save?[/b] Saves with prior air support keep using any radio; new games start the quest.
[*][b]Terminal won't open?[/b] The radio must be on and have battery. With the quest on, only the air support radio opens it.
[*][b]No aircraft in the sky?[/b] Check ZombieBuddy's Java approval and the 3D aircraft option.
[/list]
