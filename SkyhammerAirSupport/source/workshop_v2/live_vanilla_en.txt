[h1]The sky has your back. No other mod needed.[/h1]
[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813483790]Skyhammer[/url] without dependencies: unlock and call AH-64, A-10, AC-130, F-22 and B-2 support by radio. Aircraft appear as [b]ground shadows[/b], and in 3D in the terminal.

[b]Single player and multiplayer (experimental).[/b] Build 42.21+ (tested on 42.22).

[h1]How to use[/h1]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/hFfsR26y/vanilla-05-terminal.jpg[/img]
[olist]
[*]Turn on the [b]air support radio[/b] and keep it on you.
[*]Press [b]K[/b] or right-click the radio to open the terminal.
[*]Pick a tab and press [b]CALL[/b].
[*]For the 105mm, the A-10, the F-22 and the B-2, mark the target on the sensor map or in the game view ([b]EYES-ON[/b]).
[/olist]

[h2]Hellhound One-One - AH-64 Apache[/h2]

[*]Escorts you, firing 30mm at nearby infected.
[*]Rocket salvos into big groups, never near you.
[*][b]Illumination flares[/b]: light up a marked spot without drawing infected.
[/list]

[h2]Valkyrie Two-Six - A-10[/h2]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/XfPyYzjh/v2.jpg[/img]
[list]
[*][b]GAU-8 gun runs[/b]: mark one point and the pilot strafes a 40-tile line through it, in the direction that catches the most infected, never near you.
[*]Between runs, mark a new point or it flies the line back.
[*][b]Napalm[/b] on the same sortie: mark a point and a 40-tile strip goes up in flames.
[/list]

[h2]Banshee Two-One - AC-130[/h2]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/fd3FtqhZ/v3.jpg[/img]
[list]
[*][b]40mm[/b]: fires on its own at the infected around you, or where you aim it. Hold and resume fire from the terminal.
[*][b]105mm[/b]: drops shells where you mark.
[*][b]Gunner seat[/b] (single player): fire 25mm/40mm/105mm (keys 1/2/3), with a drifting thermal view and visible tracers. Targets under roofs are hidden.
[/list]

[h2]Raven One-One - F-22[/h2]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/jvK438dB/v4.jpg[/img]
[list]
[*][b]Flight strike[/b]: mark one point and ten F-22s streak over at Mach speed, 0.4 s apart, dropping 20 heavy bombs on a 70x40-tile strip, in the direction that catches the most infected.
[*]Shadows, roars, sonic booms and screen shake. Vehicles in the strip are wrecked.
[/list]

[h2]Titan Zero-One - B-2[/h2]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/qZNZ8M6/v5.jpg[/img]
[list]
[*][b]Heavy bombs[/b] carpet the area you mark.
[*]The blasts draw infected from far away.
[*][b]Tactical nuke[/b] (pick it with the weapon button before calling): mark a spot 60-300 tiles away; after 15 s, infected within 140 tiles die. Building destruction within 50 tiles requires Craters in single player or server nuclear demolition in multiplayer.
[/list]

[h2]Fallout[/h2]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/jPXGhQW1/fallout-small-en.png[/img]
[list]
[*]The nuke's ground zero stays radioactive for days. The longer you stay the sicker you get, and it can kill you even after you get out.
[*]Wear a hazmat suit or a gas mask with a filter. Prussian Blue capsules flush it out faster.
[*]NPCs get sick too.
[/list]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Timers use [b]real time[/b]; single-player pause stops them.
[*]Conventional shots and blasts only hit infected [b]outdoors[/b] (unless "Blasts reach indoors" is on). In single player the B-2's bombs go through roofs, up to three floors.
[*]Friendly [b]Bandits/A-Life[/b] NPCs are safe from called-in support and green on the sensor map; the gunner seat's guns can hit them.
[*]Tested with about 80 mods, no conflicts seen.
[/list]

[h1]Killstreak mode[/h1]
[b]Points, not cooldowns[/b]: every infected you kill yourself is a point (air support kills earn none), and points call the aircraft (Apache 80, A-10 120, AC-130 150, F-22 200, B-2 300, nuke 600). Recall before arrival for a refund. Turn it on in the sandbox or Mod Options.

[h1]Earn your air support[/h1]
New-game quest ([b]Air support unlock[/b]): build one [b]relay[/b] (one per server online): wall satellite dish + military ham radio within 6 tiles + generator power. Right-click the dish, then call Aegis Control on the [b]air support radio[/b] to unlock all five aircraft.
[img]https://i.ibb.co/Y7N1j60z/relay-420.jpg[/img]
Don't want the quest? Pick [b]Any two-way radio[/b] in the sandbox.

[h1]Multiplayer (experimental)[/h1]
[list]
[*]The server and every player need this mod.
[*]Each player calls their [b]own[/b] aircraft; the [b]nuke[/b] only if the server allows it.
[*]Cooldowns and ammo are each player's own (defaults with PvP on).
[*][b]PvP[/b]: with the sandbox's [b]Air support PvP[/b] on, air support hurts other players too, and an Apache can be shot down. Allies and indoors are safe; players get warnings. With PvP off, others only see/hear blasts.
[*][b]Fallout[/b] hurts everyone, PvP or not.
[/list]

[h1]FAQ[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Existing save?[/b] Saves with prior air support keep using any radio; new games start the quest.
[*][b]Terminal won't open?[/b] The radio must be on and have battery. With the quest on, only the air support radio opens it.
[*][b]No shadows?[/b] Outdoors only, with the shadows option on; at night only over lit ground.
[/list]
