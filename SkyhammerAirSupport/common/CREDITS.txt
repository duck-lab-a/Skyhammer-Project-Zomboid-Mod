Skyhammer Air Support - credits and asset notices

Aircraft models: the AC-130, AH-64 Apache, A-10, B-2 and F-22 (the 3D aircraft, their ground
shadows, the plain-view Apache, the 3D look-around views and the F-22's terminal picture are made
from them) are Sketchfab models under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changed for this mod: converted to the mod's own
mesh format, fewer faces, landing gear and cockpit insides taken out, texture colours baked into
the model (the A-10, AC-130 and B-2 made grey / near black), pictures rendered from them.
- AC-130: "Lockheed AC-130 "Hercules"" by helijah
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/lockheed-ac-130-hercules-361991c9874d4680931b3e0d23500e43
- AH-64 Apache: "Ah64" by manilov.ap
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ah64-3bc9b90cc97a40a6bfc13237329ce51b
- A-10: "A10" by manilov.ap
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/a10-8fec9884fe07476bbcaa34d9521726c4
- B-2: "B2" by manilov.ap

English radio audio: the aircrews' voices are generated with Chatterbox TTS (Resemble AI, MIT
license), each cloned from a short reference clip made with a Piper TTS voice (norman, bryce, joe,
kristin) or a Kokoro-82M voice (hexgrad, Apache 2.0: am_michael, am_onyx, am_echo, bm_george);
the other radio voices are Piper TTS directly. The distributed runtime includes rendered audio,
not the TTS programs or model weights. The Piper model cards list these dataset licenses:
